# Post types

Six types. Each needs a real event. The outlines are orders of content, not sentence templates; write them as normal paragraphs.

## 1. Work shown (default)

Use when a job or build is finished.

1. What the job or thing was, in one sentence with the specifics.
2. The problem it had to solve or the constraint that made it awkward.
3. What was done. One or two concrete details of method.
4. The result, with a number if a real one exists, and any limit.

For landscape or build work, a photo of the result carries half the post. Never name a client or address without the owner's say-so.

## 2. Thing built

Use for a product, template, tool or file.

1. What it is and what it does, named plainly.
2. Why it exists: the need it came from. Leave this step out if the need is not known; do not guess a motive.
3. How it works in one or two details a user would care about.
4. Its state, if known: finished, new, not yet used by customers. Leave sale details out (owner adds them).

## 3. Method note

Use when the owner has a way of doing something that others in the trade could copy.

1. The task.
2. The steps or the rule, as a short list if they are real steps.
3. Where it does not apply.

## 4. Mistake and cost

Use when something went wrong and the owner is willing to say so.

1. What went wrong, stated flat.
2. What it cost: time, money, rework. Real figures only.
3. What is done differently now. One sentence, not a sermon.

Do not use this type to boast in disguise ("my mistake was working too hard").

## 5. Trade observation

Use when a rule, price, tool or practice in the trade changed, and the owner has first-hand knowledge of the effect.

1. What changed, with the source if public.
2. What it means in practice for someone doing the work.
3. What the owner is doing about it.

Opinion with no first-hand basis is not a post.

## 6. Vlog note

Use when the owner has published a vlog about his productivity or progress.

1. What the vlog shows, in one sentence, from what he said or from the vlog itself.
2. One concrete thing from it: a result, a number, a step.
3. What did not work or is still unfinished.

The tools he used may be named here if `profile.md` allows it, plainly and without hype. He adds the link.

## Numbers for peers

When the owner wants to give other owners a figure from his own work (a count, a ratio, a cost), use this order: the figure; the rule or check that produced it; one concrete result; what the figure does not show. Include the reason behind the figure. A bare statistic followed by advice is the "lesson" post in disguise; without the reason, find it in the project files or ask for it before drafting.

## Not offered

Personal story with a lesson, motivational posts, polls, "hot takes", tributes written for reach, and announcement posts with no content beyond the announcement. The research found these are what readers mock.
